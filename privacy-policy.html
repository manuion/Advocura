Advocura Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 3, 2025

Introduction

Advocura ("we", "our", or "us") is a case management application designed for legal professionals. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and Bar Council ID when you register.
  • Case Data: Case details, client information, hearing dates, and documents you create within the app.
  • Camera Access: We request camera permission to allow you to scan documents directly into the app. Photos are only taken when you explicitly use the document scanner feature.
  • Email Data: If you choose to link your Gmail account, we access emails matching your case subject keywords to display them within the app. We only read email metadata and content - we do not modify or delete your emails.
  • Storage: Documents and emails are cached locally on your device for offline access.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the case management service
  • To sync your data across devices
  • To display case-related emails within the app
  • To send you notifications about upcoming hearings

Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services
  • Documents are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage with encryption
  • Local data on your device is stored in the app's private directory
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, database, and file storage
  • Google Gmail API: To fetch and display your case-related emails (only if you link your Gmail account)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Export your data from the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Unlink your Gmail account at any time

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Advocura is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
